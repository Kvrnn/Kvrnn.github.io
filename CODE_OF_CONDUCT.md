# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions, give constructive feedback and discuss technical disagreements without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, spam, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure others to disclose personal information or continue an unwanted interaction.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

Report concerns privately to hello@nightsquawk.tech with links and the information needed to assess the incident. Do not post private evidence publicly. If a report concerns a maintainer, identify that concern so it can be routed away from the subject where possible. A separate independent escalation contact is not currently designated.

Maintainers consider context, impact and repeated behavior. Responses may include a request to change behavior, content removal, a warning, temporary restrictions or exclusion. Reports are handled with discretion, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Good-faith reports must not lead to retaliation.
